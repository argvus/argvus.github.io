Status bar at the top or the bottom

The ARGVUS main bar combines navigation, media, system state and native apps — and can sit at the top or the bottom of the screen with a click or a shortcut. The position is remembered across sessions.

Taskbar

Built on Waybar, the bar groups everyday controls in a single place, with a look unified across every theme:

  • Top/bottom position via shortcut (SUPER + Alt + ↑/↓) or the sidebar.
  • Navigable workspaces with focused-window context.
  • Media control (mpris) with the active player highlighted.
  • Network, CPU, memory, temperature and power-profile status.
  • ARGVUS Taskbar Calendar on date click.
  • ARGVUS Removable Devices, a Rust-powered removable-storage module.
  • System tray for apps with status icons.
  • Quick app and command search right from the bar.

Native tools for the desktop

Beyond environment configuration, ARGVUS includes first-party apps for workflows that need tighter integration with the bar, panel and session.

01

ARGVUS Removable Devices

ARGVUS Removable Devices is the ARGVUS removable-storage app. It detects devices, exposes quick actions to mount, unmount and eject drives, and powers the Waybar storage module with a consistent experience.

02

ARGVUS Taskbar Calendar

ARGVUS Taskbar Calendar is the native calendar used by the desktop. It offers a quick month view, simple navigation and event integration for checking appointments without leaving the Wayland flow.

03

ARGVUS Focus

ARGVUS Focus helps you stay on task with focus sessions and breaks available directly from the desktop.

04

ARGVUS Clock

ARGVUS Clock brings a clock and timers together in a native tool, so you can keep track of time without leaving your workflow.

05

ARGVUS Control Center

ARGVUS Control Center is the native ARGVUS configuration center. Apps sets the default applications, and Fonts applies typography to GTK/Nautilus, Rofi, Waybar, Quickshell, Hyprland, the lock screen and official apps.
